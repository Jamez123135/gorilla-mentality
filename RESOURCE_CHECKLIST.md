# Free Transformation Resources: Owner Review

Archived research from October 12, 2026. The owner previously approved R02,
R03, R05, R06, R07, R09, R10, and R12. The Resources section was removed from
the app at the owner's request on October 13, 2026. Nothing here is currently
published as an app resource library; retained checkmarks record past choices.
All linked resources below are publicly accessible without a required paid
subscription. Equipment, groceries, internet access, and individual clinical
care can still have costs. Free tools cannot guarantee a transformation.

## Training And Movement

- [ ] **R01 - Strength-training principles:** [ACSM's 2026 resistance-training update](https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/). Free summary and downloadable infographic; the entire ACSM course/journal catalog is not necessarily free. Proposed use: a training reference link, not a copied article.
- [x] **R02 - Warm-up, strength and cool-down videos:** [NHS strength and resistance](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/strength-and-resistance/). Proposed use: optional links beside workouts, including warm-up, arms, legs, glutes and cool-down.
- [x] **R03 - No-gym workouts:** [NHS home workout videos](https://www.nhs.uk/better-health/get-active/home-workout-videos/). Short cardio, strengthening and stretching videos. Proposed use: a home-training resource category.
- [ ] **R04 - Beginner running:** [NHS Couch to 5K](https://www.nhs.uk/better-health/get-active/get-running-with-couch-to-5k/). Free beginner running plan. Proposed use: an optional cardio pathway, not a requirement for fat loss.
- [x] **R05 - Cardio and whole-body activity:** [CDC adult activity overview](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html). Proposed use: a reference for balancing aerobic work and strengthening all major muscle groups.

## Nutrition And Cooking

- [x] **R06 - Food and portion reference:** [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/). Free food composition search and downloadable data. Proposed use: a food-data reference link. The meal calculator already requested separately uses a small, attributed SR Legacy dataset, not a paid API.
- [x] **R07 - Healthy meal ideas:** [Canada's Food Guide recipes](https://food-guide.canada.ca/en/recipes/). Searchable recipes with ingredient and collection filters. Proposed use: recipe links to supplement the app's pantry ideas.
- [ ] **R08 - Low-cost groceries:** [Health Canada: healthy eating on a budget](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-guide/eating-support/meal-planning-grocery-shopping/healthy-eating-budget.html). Proposed use: a budget and shopping resource alongside meal planning.
- [x] **R09 - Meal-prep safety:** [FoodSafety.gov food safety charts](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts). Cooking temperatures and cold-storage guidance. Proposed use: a safety link beside meal-prep suggestions.

## Recovery And Consistency

- [x] **R10 - Sleep and recovery:** [CDC: about sleep](https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/index.html). Age-specific sleep guidance and sleep habits. Proposed use: a recovery reference, not a diagnosis or sleep-treatment tool.
- [ ] **R11 - Stress management:** [NHS breathing exercises for stress](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/breathing-exercises-for-stress/). Proposed use: a simple optional recovery resource, not a substitute for mental-health care.
- [x] **R12 - Habits and maintenance:** [NIDDK: changing your habits for better health](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diet-nutrition/changing-habits-better-health). Proposed use: guidance for realistic goals, barriers, and sustaining routines.

## Optional Adult Planning Tool

- [ ] **R13 - Weight-goal planning:** [NIDDK Body Weight Planner](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/weight-management/body-weight-planner). Free adult planning tool. Only for adults 18+, not for pregnancy or breastfeeding. Proposed use: an optional external link, with those restrictions clearly shown; not automatic calorie changes.

## Integration Rules

Approved resources will be organized by training, nutrition, recovery and
consistency. Membership and tracking remain free; only personal 1:1 coaching
is paid and arranged directly. Link to the original resource rather than
scraping or republishing copyrighted articles or videos. Record review dates
and recheck links periodically. Do not promise spot reduction, rapid results,
or medically personalized treatment. Refer injuries, medical conditions,
pregnancy, breastfeeding, eating-disorder concerns, and under-18 nutrition
planning to an appropriately qualified professional.

Open `resource-checklist.html` for a clickable version. Its checkmarks stay
in your browser and do not automatically approve or deploy app changes.
